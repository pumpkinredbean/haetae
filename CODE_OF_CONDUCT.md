# Code of conduct

## Expected behavior

Project participants must communicate respectfully, critique work with
specific evidence, acknowledge uncertainty, protect private data, and make
research limitations visible. Harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted
sexual attention, deliberate exposure of private information, and sustained
disruption are unacceptable.

## Scope

This policy applies in repository discussions, review threads, project events,
and private communication made in a project role.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns through a private maintainer contact published in the
repository settings. If no private contact is available, open a minimal issue
requesting one and include no sensitive details. Maintainers will limit access
to the report, avoid conflicts of interest, and may remove content, restrict
participation, or ban a participant when needed to protect the community. A
separate conduct-reporting contact must be published before the first public
release.

Retaliation against a person who reports a concern or assists an investigation
is prohibited.
